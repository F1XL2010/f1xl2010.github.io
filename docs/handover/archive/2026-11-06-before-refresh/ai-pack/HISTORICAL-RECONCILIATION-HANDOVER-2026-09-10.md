## Definition of done

Historical reconciliation is complete when:

- every season has a recorded outcome at all three audit layers;
- confirmed transfer errors have been repaired and rechecked;
- uncertainty is explicitly recorded rather than filled with conjecture;
- intentional differences are documented and no longer repeatedly flagged;
- final tables and accolades match the owner's authoritative records or carry an
  explicit owner-approved override;
- no unresolved result discrepancy can silently distort driver or constructor
  statistics;
- the owner has a final season-by-season exception list;
- work can return to the FAP without another general historical re-audit.
