Now That's What I Call F1XL
In the summer of 2025 we released an album entitled "Now That's What I Call F1XL". All of the tracks were dedicated to, inspired by and written about our community, the league and the members therein. Using AI to create music to accompany human written lyrics they were later accompanied by fun AI generated music videos. All of this was with tongue very much placed firmly in cheek.
