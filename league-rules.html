League Rules
F1XL Management reserve the right to amend this document at any time. All changes will be communicated via Discord.
From the Admins
This document has been created to provide drivers old and new full details of the league racing we provide here at F1XL. It also provides information as to what drivers can expect from F1XL, and in turn what F1XL expects from any driver that signs up to race here in terms of standards both on the track, and off it.

We strive to provide an environment that is welcoming to all, with clean fair racing. Whilst we appreciate the Rules here are extensive, we believe they are necessary to ensure that standards are high in all areas and, most importantly, that everyone has fun.

Please take the time to read all the rules and familiarise yourself with them before racing. Not being aware of them is not a suitable defence for any on or off track incidents.
Section 1–6
General Information
▼
1
Joining the League
a
We are a cross platform league, though predominantly Xbox based. Drivers looking to join the league must sign up to the Discord and complete a time trial so that we may assess and place them in a division according to their results.
b
We try to run two complete seasons per game cycle (approximately 12 months). For the first season after release, all drivers are required to complete a time trial (see section 16). The second season does not require a time trial unless you have either not raced in the previous season or completed 3 races or less during said season. League placement for those who have not needed to do a time trial will be based on the previous season's performance.
c
Key dates will be communicated prior to each season and placed on Discord.
d
Once the divisions have been announced and drivers placed, signups will re-open and we accept applications again for that season. However, any drivers signing at this point will not be guaranteed to be able to race every week as drivers already signed up will be given priority. This can change if drivers drop out.
2
Acknowledgements
By signing up to this league, drivers are expected to:
a
Commit to the entirety of the season, unless otherwise stated at application.
b
Understand and comply with the rules as set out on this page.
c
Remain on the F1XL Discord server during the entirety of the season. Leaving the Discord will be considered as leaving the league.
Leaving the league mid-season, without an adequate reason, will result in a mark against you which could result in restricted participation in future seasons or even refused entry.
3
Dead Heat
If two or more teams/drivers finish the season with the same number of points, the higher place in the Championship shall be awarded to:
a
The holder of the greatest number of first places.
b
If the number of first places is the same, the holder of the greatest number of second places.
c
If the number of first and second places is the same, the holder of the greatest number of third places and so on until a winner emerges.
4
Gamertag / PSN / EA ID Change
Drivers are allowed to change their name on their specific platform as well as on Discord. However, platform name changes must be advised to F1XL Management and reflected accordingly on your Discord profile.
5
Recording and Streaming
a
Drivers are not required to record their full race.
b
Drivers submitting footage for tickets or appeals must not delete footage until the appeals process has been completed at the earliest.
c
Drivers are allowed to stream their races but should only do so if their internet allows them to. If an incident was to be caused by a driver's lag, this driver will be deemed responsible and thus might be awarded a penalty.
d
All commentated races will be streamed on any of the F1XL Twitch or YouTube channels. Confirmation will be sent prior to the race.
6
Attendance
a
5 days prior to a race, an attendance poll will open with 3 options: available, unavailable and tentative. Drivers are requested to answer it as quickly as possible. Based on that poll, the grid will be made up and reserve drivers allocated a seat.
b
Drivers selecting Tentative should update their vote no later than a couple of hours prior to the race, to ensure seats are filled by primary drivers rather than reserves.
c
A driver that fails to attend a race without notification and/or a valid reason for 3 concurrent weeks may be removed from their team by the Team Principal.
d
Un-notified, sporadic attendance or quitting the league early may impact authorised future participation. F1XL Management may disallow entry or restrict to reserve status only.
e
The latest point at which a driver may sign up is 8.30pm on the day of the race. Drivers that sign up after that time may not be allowed participation in that evening's race.
Section 7–13
League Structure
▼
7
General Info
The league is split into multiple divisions with driver placement determined by time trials. There is no promotion or relegation at the end of a season, but drivers may be moved depending on number of sign ups and previous league performances. Teams are determined by the selection of Team Principals who participate in a draft to pick their drivers. Team Principals then manage their teams over the course of the season.
8
Champions
The driver/team that accumulates the most points over the season is declared Champion in each division, whilst the Team Principal with the combined most constructors' points is declared as the overall Champion.
9
Classification / Points System
a
All drivers who completed 90% of the total race distance will be classified even if attempting to rejoin still at the time of race completion.
b
A driver who disconnected and did not rejoin after covering 90% of the race distance will be classified where their AI finished.
c
Points follow the standard F1 system of 25 per win, 18 for second and so on. A point is awarded for fastest lap for any driver in the top ten. Sprint races follow real F1 scoring of 8 to 1.
10
Mid-Season Moves
A driver can be promoted or demoted at any point in the season at the discretion of F1XL Management. We will try to ensure all moves are done as early in the season as possible to avoid disruption, but we reserve the right to move a driver at any time if the need is valid.
11
Race Timings / Lobby Information
a
All divisions run to the same timing plan. Races are held weekly, every Sunday. Exceptions include festive holidays and occasions where a real life F1 race is at the same time.
b
Official practice sessions start at 8pm BST and run for 45 minutes.
c
Official Qualifying sessions start at 9pm BST and run for 18 minutes (Short qualifying).
d
Official Sprint/Race sessions start right after the Qualifying session is finished.
e
At the discretion of the Race Controllers, a 5-minute leeway is given for drivers running late. All sessions must start no later than 5 minutes after the official start time.
f
We advise all drivers to hard reset your console and router before any race session to reduce any chance of lag.
g
A minimum of 10 drivers is required for the race to start. A race with less than 10 drivers may be postponed or cancelled at the discretion of the League Co-ordinators.
h
Drivers must ensure they are in the right car when the session is about to start. Starting the session in the wrong car will lead to a post-race penalty.
i
Only the drivers and commentators are allowed in the lobby.
12
Set Ups
a
The use of custom setups in F1XL is not allowed.
b
You may use any of the 5 default setups.
c
You may only change parts of your setup that the game allows you to (fuel level for the race, front wing angle at the first pitstop, differential and brake bias out on track etc.)
d
F1XL Management may adapt or change these rules depending on the status of the game and the functionality of its systems. This will be communicated at the first opportunity.
e
Drivers are expected to use the correct tyre compound during qualifying and races. Going on track on intermediates or wets in dry conditions, or going on dry tyres in the wet, is NOT allowed.

Section 14–20
Teams, Drivers, Tiers & Team Principals
▼
14
Team Principals
Duties include, but are not limited to:
a
Selecting drivers during the preseason draft.
b
Managing their drivers' needs during the season and maintaining their discipline on track and off.
c
Relaying information from F1XL Management to their drivers and ensuring they understand.
d
Reviewing and submitting the weekly tickets on behalf of their drivers. Tickets submitted that do not meet the criteria or clearly show there is no issue will be rejected and the TP runs the risk of receiving a warning.
e
Providing a separate Discord for their team.
How TPs run their team is up to them, but F1XL standards are expected to be maintained. Multiple complaints from drivers about their TP may result in an investigation and potential removal from their position.
15
Tiers
Within each team there are 2 tiers. TPs can only have 1 driver from each tier racing for their team at any one time in each division. If a team needs a tier 1 driver but no such driver is available, they may be allowed to sign a tier 2 driver in a tier 1 slot but may not sign a tier 1 driver to a tier 2 slot.
16
Time Trials
Time trials are used to determine which division and tier each driver should be placed into. Drivers may record themselves or book an appointment with the F1XL time trial team.
a
Own Recording
i
Time trials are done in a multiplayer race lobby and the driver will need another person with them to start the race.
ii
The second person will need to retire from the race when it starts.
iii
Videos need to start on the main menu and show all lobby settings.
iv
The driver has the choice of submitting one video, or two separate ones.
v
During the race, the driver must not use overtake, but they are permitted to adjust brake bias and differential.
vi
Settings: Multiplayer → Social Play → Create Custom Game → Friends/Invite Only. Tracks: Great Britain and Australia. Car: Equal F1 25 McLaren. Session: Short (5 laps). Setup: Fixed. No braking or steering assists. Weather: Clear. Damage: Off. Corner cutting: Strict. Collisions: Off. ERS: Off. Session start time: Realistic.
b
Time Trial Team — Drivers may be accompanied by a member of the Time Trial team who will set up the lobby themselves and watch on spectator.
c
Appointments & Submission — Both appointments and submissions can be arranged on the relevant Discord sections.
F1XL Management would encourage all parties to do so to the best of their abilities. Intentionally misrepresenting your own pace will ultimately just result in you being moved and disrupting your own season.
17
Draft Night
During the draft each round is completed by TPs and tier 1 and tier 2 drivers are picked for each league. TPs will announce their picks to specified parties who will then announce on the F1XL Draft Livestream. Full instructions will be presented on the evening.
18
Leaving a Team — Driver
At any point in the season, a driver may leave their team. Any driver leaving a team must drive for a reserve team for a minimum of 2 races before being able to join another team. DNF or DSQ counts towards the 2 races unless subject to a disciplinary ticket. DNS does not count.
19
Firing a Driver — Team Principal
a
A TP may release a driver at any point. The TP may not sign another driver until 2 races have passed. The released driver may join another team immediately.
b
If a driver fails to show up for 3 races in a row, they can be released by their TP immediately.
c
If a driver is released due to misconduct (section 45), the TP may sign any eligible free agent immediately.
d
If a driver is released due to inactivity, the TP may sign any eligible driver immediately.
20
Reserve Drivers
Reserve drivers fall into two categories: full time drivers not selected for a team, or part time drivers who will not be competing every race.
a
Part Time Drivers — May sign up to each individual race but priority is always given to full time drivers. Part time drivers may still be signed by Team Principals and are subject to the same rules as any other driver.
b
Full Time Reserve Drivers — Full time drivers without an allocated team default into a reserve team. They are eligible to be signed by a TP after the season begins and have priority over part time drivers for participation.
Section 21–39
On Track
▼
21
Qualifying
a
Giving a tow to any driver from a different team is prohibited. Both the towing driver and the towed driver may be penalised.
b
Drivers catching slower cars are allowed to move into their slipstream.
c
If a driver is awarded what they believe to be an unfair grid place penalty, they must contact the League Co-ordinators as quickly as possible.
d
No restart will be made if a driver spins and is disqualified for stopping in a dangerous place.
e
Cars not on a flying lap must make every effort not to interfere with other vehicles. Qualifying interference includes: obstruction, visual impairment, giving dirty air, or losing control and forcing evasive actions.
f
If a driver is being obstructed, they must make every reasonable effort to avoid causing a crash. Failure to do so may result in a penalty.
g
Purposefully crashing during qualifying is a serious offence. You MUST record the incident if you crash out of qualifying.
h
You may only retire whilst inside your pit box during qualifying. Retiring out on track makes you liable for any resulting tickets.
i
If there is not enough time to start a final push lap, drivers should not leave the pits as the AI will take control when the timer hits 0:00.
22
Qualifying Ban
a
Drivers serving a qualifying ban are allowed to do an "installation lap" — leave the pits and go straight back in without crossing the start/finish line.
b
If the lobby is reset between qualifying and the race, a driver having served their qualifying ban will be put at the back of the grid.
23
Qualifying Tyres
Drivers are expected to use the correct tyre compound during qualifying. Going on track on intermediates or wets in dry conditions, or going on dry tyres in the wet, is NOT allowed.
24
Race Tyres
a
During a dry race, all drivers are required to use at least 2 different dry tyre compounds, failure to do so will result in a disqualification. If wet tyres are required during the race, the previous rule no longer applies.
b
Going on track on intermediates or wets in dry conditions, or going on dry tyres in the wet, is NOT allowed.
25
Formation Lap & Grid Start
a
The driver starting from pole position will set the pace.
b
Drivers must remain within roughly 10 car lengths of the car ahead.
c
Deliberately crashing on the formation lap is not allowed and will result in a severe penalty.
d
Intentionally hitting another driver under formation lap conditions is not permitted.
e
The Race Controllers decide when to press 'A' to begin the race. Any driver pressing it before this time will receive a reprimand + 5 seconds penalty.
26
Pit Entry, Pit Lane, Pit Exit
a
The section of track between the first safety car line and the beginning of the pit lane is designated the "pit entry".
b
The section of track between the end of the pit lane and the second safety car line is designated the "pit exit".
c
Any driver intending to enter the pits must make sure it is safe to do so and must not hinder or unreasonably endanger other cars.
d
When all four tyres are fully across the pit entry line, the driver must not re-enter the track and must continue into the pits.
e
Any driver exiting the pits must not cross the white line.
f
Rules 26c, 26d and 26e can be disregarded by the stewards in cases of uncontrollable events.
g
If a track has a temporary change to these rules for a specific race, these will be communicated prior to the race. Not being aware of announced temporary rules is not a valid defence.
27
Starting the Race
Drivers must start the race by themselves. Starting the race in AI mode will automatically result in a warning. If any advantage is gained, a more severe penalty will be awarded.
28
Track Limits
a
The white lines that define the track edges are considered to be part of the track, but the kerbs are not.
b
Drivers must make every reasonable effort to stay on the track at all times and may not deliberately leave the track without justifiable reason.
c
A driver will be judged to have left the track if no part of their car remains in contact with the track.
d
A driver who has left the track may rejoin only if it is safe to do so and without gaining an advantage.
e
If a driver is deemed to have gained an advantage by leaving the track, they must make every effort to give back the whole of any advantage gained.
29
Resetting to the Track
Drivers are allowed to reset to the track as long as they do not endanger any other driver. A track reset is deemed dangerous when a driver rejoins in front of another, irrespective of whether you are ghosted or not.
30
Track Furniture
a
Intentionally taking out brake marker boards is considered unsportsmanlike behaviour and is prohibited.
b
Drivers will not be penalised for impacting and displacing any bollard on the circuit.
31
Driving Standards
a
At no time may a car be driven unnecessarily slowly, erratically, or in any manner that may be deemed potentially dangerous.
b
Drivers are allowed to go into AI (by pausing the game) for a limited amount of time but they are always responsible for what their car does.
32
Overtaking and Defending
a
Overtaking may be done from the left or the right.
b
More than one change of direction to defend a position is not permitted.
c
At the beginning of a straight, a driver may "break the tow" by moving off-line and getting back onto the racing line once without it being considered a defensive manoeuvre.
d
Any driver defending their position on a straight may use the full width of the track during their first move providing there is no significant portion of the opposing car alongside them. A significant portion is defined as any part of the front wheel ahead of the rear axle of the car in front.
e
Defensive manoeuvres should always be proactive. Reactive defending and blocking are strictly prohibited.
f
A driver having defended their position off-line may move back towards the racing line but must leave a car width of space between them and the edge of the track.
g
Moving under braking is prohibited and may result in a penalty.
h
Deliberate crowding of a car beyond the edge of the track or any abnormal change in direction such as weaving on a straight is not permitted.
i
If a driver leaves the track whilst defending their position, they may be awarded a penalty for leaving the track and gaining an advantage.
j
When 2 drivers are fighting side-by-side through a corner, the defending driver is expected to leave racing room to the overtaking driver as long as they remain sufficiently alongside. A driver is deemed alongside when their front axle is in front of the other driver's rear axle.
k
Causing collisions to gain advantages is prohibited. Running into the side of another car on the exit of a corner to hinder them is prohibited.
l
An overtaking car must take reasonable effort to avoid contact and must not make dangerous or unnecessary manoeuvres to pass.
m
A defending car must be aware of when to concede their position. Any action deemed dangerous may be penalised.
n
If a driver's car is ghosted, they must continue to race as though it is not. Using the situation to their advantage by driving purposefully through other cars is prohibited.
o
This list is by no means exhaustive and F1XL Management reserve the right to amend and update as needed.
33
Giving Back a Position
a
A driver looking to give a position away should first make sure it is safe to do so.
b
When a driver gives up a position gained illegally, letting the other driver get ahead is not sufficient — they are expected to end the wheel-to-wheel battle before attempting another overtake.
c
In case of illegal overtakes, drivers must give back the position at the earliest opportunity.
d
Drivers should not slow down and wait to let an overtaken driver through, even as a sportsmanship act. Doing so will not be taken into consideration by the stewards.
34
Lapped Cars
a
As soon as a driver is caught by another about to lap them, they must allow the faster driver through at the earliest opportunity. They have the current and the entirety of the next sector to do so.
b
Any driver deemed to have deliberately blocked or hindered the faster car will be severely penalised.
c
Lapped cars are allowed to unlap themselves if their pace allows them to create a gap in a reasonable amount of time. However, they are not allowed to battle the lapping car.
35
Safety Car / Virtual Safety Car
a
When the SC/VSC is deployed, drivers must respect the SC/VSC Delta. Failure to do so may result in a penalty.
b
If a driver overtakes another as the Safety Car is deployed, they must give up the position(s) gained if the move was clearly completed after the "Safety Car deployed" message is displayed.
c
At no time should a driver be more than 2 seconds under the delta unless in exceptional circumstances.
d
Drivers must drive safely under SC/VSC. Sudden changes of direction or heavy braking may be penalised.
e
Overtaking under SC/VSC is prohibited except: if signalled by the game; when entering/exiting the pits relative to the safety car lines; when the SC is returning to pits and has passed the first SC line; whilst in the pit area; or if a car noticeably slows due to an issue.
f
Letting other drivers through under SC/VSC conditions is prohibited unless under glitches or mitigating circumstances.
g
Under Safety Car conditions, drivers must remain within 10 car-lengths of the car in front.
h
Drivers not having caught up to the queue must always try to rejoin it as quickly as possible.
i
Until the restart, drivers are not allowed to go alongside one another.
j
When the Safety Car is about to come in, the leader may dictate the pace and fall back more than 10 car-lengths. Once the leader decides to restart they are not allowed to slow down again — start-and-stops are prohibited.
k
When the Safety Car period ends, overtaking before the start/finish line is prohibited.
l
After the Safety Car pits, the lead car must not reduce speed below 50mph / 80kph.
36
Cheating
a
Cheating is strictly prohibited and will result in an indefinite ban.
b
If a driver is subject to a glitch and gains an advantage out of it, they are expected to give it up as soon as possible.
c
Abusing any game glitch is prohibited and may be punished.
d
If a driver is found to have cheated, they will be disqualified from all races they have taken part in.
e
Drivers must drive for themselves and their own team. Any cooperation between drivers from different teams is strictly prohibited.
f
Deliberately crashing is not allowed and will result in a severe penalty.
g
Deliberately causing another driver severe damage or a DNF will result in a severe penalty up to and including a permanent ban.
h
Deliberate attempts to circumvent any perceived loopholes or temporary rule changes will result in severe penalties.
37
Game Related Issues
a
Disconnections during Qualifying — Drivers may rejoin during the session. If unable to rejoin, the lobby carries on and the driver may try to join again for the race. If multiple drivers disconnect, the lobby may be restarted with a new host.
b
Disconnections between Qualifying and Race — League Co-ordinators may set up a new lobby using the grid editor. Prior qualifying results will stand.
c
Disconnections during the Race — Drivers may rejoin and retake control. The race will not be restarted due to a disconnection. If a driver cannot rejoin before the end, they will be awarded full points as long as 75% of the race has been completed.
38
Collapsed, Split or Glitched Lobby
a
Actions taken are at the discretion of the League Co-ordinators.
b
Less than 50% completed: postponement or full restart may be called. 50–75% completed: race result called with half points. Over 75% completed: race result called with full points.
39
Reset Lobby
a
Weather settings for a new race lobby will be based on the original race conditions using custom weather settings. If qualifying is restarted, dynamic weather will be used.
b
If the session fails to start within 50 minutes of the original scheduled start time, the event will be abandoned and either postponed or cancelled.
c
If a lobby is reset in qualifying, a full qualifying should be undertaken if noted within the first 5 minutes. If noted after 5 minutes, the original session is void and a one-shot qualifying session will be undertaken.
Section 40–46
Stewarding, Tickets & Appeals
▼
40
Stewards Briefing
a
Shortly before every race the Stewards will advise the Team Principals of any outstanding penalties, who will then relay it to their drivers.
b
Any ruleset changes will be relayed to TPs and the wider community. These temporary changes will override relevant sections in the League Rules.
41
Submitting Tickets
If a driver is involved in an incident they deem worthy of raising, this is done via their Team Principal who will submit the ticket on their behalf.
a
The ticket should be submitted via the appropriate page on this website.
b
The deadline is 9pm the day immediately following the race (Monday).
c
Any ticket without evidence will be rejected. The more evidence provided, the better.
d
Video evidence submitted via clips on phone are not permitted.
e
Car contact incidents and qualifying interference can only be reported by the drivers involved. If replay or highlights footage is used, it MUST show at least 1x speed.
f
For incidents of other types (illegal overtake, pit infringements, track limits etc.), they can be reported by any driver with any sort of footage.
42
In-Game Penalty Removal
a
Track limits warnings will not be removed except under certain circumstances, accompanied with video evidence and the FULL race director.
b
In case of multiple warnings for the same incident, the full lap must be provided as well as the full race director.
c
If awarded an overly harsh corner cutting penalty, a driver may request removal with video evidence and FULL race director. The penalty may be removed or converted to a warning.
d
If awarded a penalty for other reasons, the driver must provide video evidence and prove it was not served in the pits.
43
Penalties
Drivers deemed to have caused an incident may be awarded the following post-race penalties:
i
Time Penalties
ii
Warnings
iii
Reprimands
iv
Qualifying Ban
v
Race Ban
vi
Expulsion from season
vii
Ban from F1XL
Both the ticket and appeals team have the option to add additional time penalties to any of the above.

45
Misconduct
a
No deliberate "flaming" or "trolling".
b
No bigotry of any kind. Any kind of hate speech, sexism, racism, or attacks against the LGBTQ+ community will result in an immediate ban.
c
Please keep any discourse polite. Personal attacks will not be tolerated.
d
Feel free to discuss incidents on track, but we encourage clear-headedness. If you cannot do this, submit your ticket and leave no comment in the chat.
e
No inappropriate, offensive or illegal content posted on Discord directly or via link. This includes inappropriate nicknames.
f
Political and religious discussion is allowed, however abusing or belittling someone because their views differ from yours is not.
g
No spamming.
h
No impersonating other members.
i
If you are banned from F1XL, any attempts to evade or circumvent this ban by joining under an alternative identity will result in the new account being immediately banned.
j
F1XL is not responsible for any content posted on Discord by members. If you take offence to any content, please message a member of F1XL Management.
46
Appeal Process
Appeals are for both disagreements with ticket results or requests to remove an in-game penalty.
a
The appeal deadline is by default 24 hours after ticket results are posted, but can be extended at F1XL Management's discretion.
b
Appeals are to be submitted to a driver's TP who will lodge the appeal on their behalf.
c
Appeals are lodged in the same way as regular tickets, clearly stating that it is an appeal.
d
For an incident to be reviewed again, the driver must either bring new relevant evidence or highlight a mistake in the findings. Footage not submitted in time will NOT count. Failure to do so will result in the appeal being dismissed.
e
The Head Steward may refuse to open an appeal thread if the appeal is for the wrong reasons or the decision is extremely unlikely to be overturned.
f
Appeal verdicts are final.
