All Star Race
The top drivers from each division compete in an end-of-season showpiece event
The F1XL All Star Race brings together the top performers from every division at the end of each season. Teams are specially selected and differ from the main season, with a unique points system: 20 points for the winner down to 1 point for 20th. Tracks are chosen at random.
