About F1XL

Multiplatform racing

Xbox, PlayStation 5, and PC all race together. We use preset settings so no one is left behind by hardware differences.

Team Principals

Uniquely, every season features Team Principals who draft drivers into their teams — broadcast live on Twitch. There's nothing else like it.

A division for everyone

With 4 to 6 divisions each season, newcomers and veterans alike find their level. Promotion and relegation keeps competition fierce.

Global community

Based in the UK but racing worldwide — drivers from the USA, Romania, South Africa, Canada, Australia and beyond have all raced with F1XL.
