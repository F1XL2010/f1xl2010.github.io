Applications
Applications are always open — select the relevant form below
F1 Driver Application
Apply to race in one of our F1 divisions. New drivers will be required to complete a time trial so we can assess and place you in the right division. See the League Rules for full details on the time trial process.
F2 Driver Application
Apply to race in the F2 category. Complete the form below and we'll be in touch with next steps via Discord.
Staff Application
Interested in joining the F1XL team as a steward, race controller, commentator or in another capacity? Fill in the form below and tell us what you can bring to the league.
Time Trial Submission
Submit your time trial results here. Before submitting, make sure your recording follows the requirements set out in Section 16 of the League Rules — videos must start on the main menu showing all lobby settings, and overtake must not be used during the trial.
