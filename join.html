Why F1XL

Racing that works for everyone

Most sim racing leagues are intimidating — custom setups, strict experience requirements, highly competitive entry barriers. F1XL is different. We've been running since the very beginning in 2010 with the sole aim of creating a fun league, aimed at casual and competitive alike, where people without the time to dedicate blood, sweat and tears to practising can just show up and race. You can also be assured that the league is well run, with a robust rule set in place to ensure everyone is treated fairly and races are conducted in the right spirit.

🎮
No custom setups
Race with standard in-game settings. No custom assists configurations required — just the game as it comes.
🏎️
Multiple divisions
From competitive to casual. Time trials place you in the right division so you're always racing people at your level.
🤝
Team structure
Every driver races under a Team Principal who manages the team, handles tickets, and looks after their drivers — like a proper F1 team.

From application to race night

1
Explore the site
Check out the standings, results, Hall of Fame and team history. Get a feel for the league and what F1XL is about before you apply.

3
Join the Discord
The F1XL Discord is where everything happens — announcements, community chat, and where you submit your time trial to get placed in a division.
Join Discord →
4
Track Draw — live on Twitch
Before each season, the race calendar is revealed live on Twitch. Team Principals are announced and the season build-up begins.
Watch on Twitch →
5
Draft Night — live on Twitch
The highlight of the pre-season. Team Principals pick their drivers live on Twitch — find out which team you'll be racing for and who your teammates are.

6
Race on Sunday nights
Every Sunday night, all four divisions race. Full race weekends, stewards, race directors, ticket system — a proper league experience from start to finish.
Platforms

Cross-platform racing

F1XL started as an Xbox league in 2010 — hence the name. But as the F1 game went cross-platform, so did we. Today our drivers race across all three platforms in the same divisions.
